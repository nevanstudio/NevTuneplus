# Code of Conduct

NevTune+ is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone involved in the project. Whether you are a contributor, maintainer, or community member, everyone is expected to help create a positive and productive experience.

## 1. Expected Behavior

All participants in the NevTune+ community are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully, professionally, and constructively.
- Treat contributors, maintainers, and users with courtesy and consideration.
- Welcome different perspectives, ideas, and experiences.
- Keep discussions focused on the project's goals and technical improvements.
- Accept constructive feedback and criticism professionally.
- Collaborate in good faith and contribute to a supportive community.
- Respect the time, effort, and contributions of others.

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated within the NevTune+ community:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, offensive, threatening, or derogatory language.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated inappropriate conduct.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or inappropriate comments.
- Sharing private or confidential information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately undermining, intimidating, or discouraging other contributors.
- Any conduct that creates a hostile, unsafe, or unwelcoming environment.

## 3. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

**Contact information:**
- Email: nevanherris@gmail.com
- GitHub: [@nevanstudio](https://github.com/nevanstudio)

Please include relevant details or evidence where possible to help with the review.

All reports will be handled with discretion, confidentiality where reasonably possible, and appropriate attention. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith will not be tolerated.

## 4. Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, the maintainer reserves the right to:

- Issue a warning or request that inappropriate behavior stop.
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, discussions, or pull requests that violate these guidelines.
- Temporarily restrict participation in project discussions or repositories.
- Permanently remove or block individuals from participating in the project.
- Take any additional reasonable action necessary to protect the community and maintain a constructive environment.

Enforcement decisions will consider the severity, frequency, and impact of the reported behavior.

## 5. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all NevTune+ project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces officially associated with the project.

By participating in NevTune+, you agree to follow these guidelines and contribute to a respectful and collaborative environment.
